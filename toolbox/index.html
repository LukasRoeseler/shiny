Welcome to the Toolbox Website!

Migrated from the former Shiny app at metaanalyses.shinyapps.io/toolbox/ → now hosted at lukasroeseler.github.io/shiny/toolbox/. The p-curve and z-curve analyses are computed live in R in your browser (WebR).

What is this toolbox for?

A considerable proportion of social science research may not replicate. This website contains a toolbox to determine the trustworthiness of a selected set of findings.

How to use it?

P-Curve

The p-curve analysis (Simonsohn et al., 2014) assesses the p-value distribution. If the null hypothesis is true, p-values will be distributed uniformly; if false, p-values will be right-skewed toward zero. The p-curve below is computed live in R from your processed dataset.

Z-Curve

Caliper Test

A caliper test (Gerber & Malhotra, 2008) compares the number of z-scores in a narrow band below and above the significance threshold (e.g., z = 1.96) using a binomial test.

Relative Proximity

The relative proximity (RP) index (Götz et al., 2021) is the ratio of a confidence interval's proximity to zero to its width. CIs that exclude (include) zero yield a positive (negative) RP. The threshold RP = 0 should not induce inconsistencies; selection biases shift more values to the positive side. A binomial test compares the number of positive and negative RPs in a band around 0.
