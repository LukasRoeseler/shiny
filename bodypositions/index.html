Methods

Multilevel random-effects model (random = ~1 | id2/id1, ML with Knapp–Hartung adjustment). Publication-bias analyses include trim-and-fill, p-uniform*, Hedges–Vevea selection model, PET–PEESE, p-curve and z-curve. R packages used: metafor, meta, robumeta, puniform, weightr, zcurve, dmetar (p-curve).
