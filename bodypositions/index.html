Dynamic Meta-Analysis of Body Position Effects

Röseler, L., Körner, R., & Schütz, A. (2021) · Version v0.72 · Last update: 07 July 2022

About

Migrated from the former Shiny app at metaanalyses.shinyapps.io/bodypositions/ → now hosted at lukasroeseler.github.io/shiny/bodypositions/.

This interactive app presents a meta-analysis of experimentally induced body position (posture/pose) effects on behavioral, self-report, and physiological dependent variables. The original Shiny app allowed researchers to filter the set of studies and add new studies; this static version presents the precomputed meta-analytic results.

Citation: Röseler, L., Körner, R., & Schütz, A. (2021). Dynamic Meta-Analysis. https://osf.io/ns65r/

Data and Materials: https://osf.io/vz4a6/ · Add study: Submission portal
